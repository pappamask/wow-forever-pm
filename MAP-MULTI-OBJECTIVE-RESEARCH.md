# Multi-objective marker research — all 29 route places

Implementation note: this audit captured the map before the current marker changes. The subsequent source edits add distinct named objective stops where the available evidence supports a landmark, and leave uncertain locations explicitly approximate or unplaced.

30 September 2026. Research sidecar only; no source data or UI was changed. I compared `src/guide-data.json` (`earlyQuestClusters`), `src/map-data.json` (`laterClusters`), `src/map-positions.json`, `src/map-sites.json`, `src/map-hubs.json`, and the 1000×668 map art. The 29 keys are route places, **not** 29 distinct outdoor maps: Northshire, Shadowglen and Coldridge use parent-zone art; six keys are dungeon/travel contexts. This note identifies where one cluster center wrongly appears to locate several objectives.

**Evidence rule:** `A(x,y)` below is an *approximate pixel on the existing artwork*, not an in-game coordinate or verified spawn. A named place in a Forever quest's actual description/objective and the beta client's map/AreaTable is stronger than an inherited Classic comment or waypoint. WCLBox world X/Y is useful for the entity listed, but cannot be pasted into artwork pixels. `src/map-positions.json` itself calls its points planning-area centers. A service point, giver, quest target, instance portal and interior goal must not share a precision claim. ## P0: Dun Morogh is demonstrably wrong as a single objective point

`dunmorogh:steelgrill-farsen` is currently one objective marker at `A(625,330)`, between Steelgrill's Depot and Earthseer Farsen. `dunmorogh:dark-iron-map` is at `A(556,210)`, near Ironforge, even though its map-drop fight is **southeast**. The source guide itself joins pickup, farsight, spy fight, map turn-in and dungeon approach into two composite clusters.

The [Forever Farsen's Watch quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96392/farsens-watch) requires *using Farsen's farsight* with Farsen as both start and end—not fighting at the observed distant location. [Nip 'Em in the Bud](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96390/nip-em-in-the-bud) explicitly sends the player east to **Ironband's Compound** for 10 Dark Iron Spies. [Underground Map](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96391/underground-map) is begun by the dropped map and returned to Farsen. The [Forever Dark Iron Map item](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=274268/dark-iron-map) lists Dark Iron Spy/Captain Beld as drops; the item-page location comments corroborate the compound but are player observations, not exact beta pin evidence. The [Forever Dun Morogh client map](https://warcraftforever.games/maps/dun-morogh) includes Ironband's Compound as a separate subzone. The older [Classic Ironband's Compound quest](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1681/ironbands-compound) also says south of Helm's Bed Lake; use this only as corroboration of the named landmark, not as a Forever coordinate.

Do **not** move `Farsen's Watch` objective to Ironband's Compound merely because the farsight camera views it. The *following* quest sends the player there. The guide prose “nearby investigation objective” should eventually be corrected to this distinction. If the UI cannot distinguish a quest's pickup and objective marker under one cluster, the safe immediate fix is to label the old composite as a *route circuit*, and add the named Ironband's Compound objective rather than claiming the center is exact.

## Suggested implementation order / verification

1. **Ship the Dun Morogh semantic fix first:** attach `hall-farsens-watch` to Farsen (farsight interaction), `hall-nip-bud` and the dropped-map objective to Ironband's Compound, and keep Farsen turn-in and Hall entrance distinct. Preserve existing quest IDs and progress keys. The single old `dark-iron-map` pin near Ironforge is misleading for the outdoor fight.
2. Add multiple named objective points to the early composite clusters with clear named landmarks: Elwynn farms/mines/lake; Teldrassil lake/den/glade; Westfall farms/mine/coast/Moonbrook; Redridge lake/canyons/Stonewatch; Duskwood Gardens/Raven Hill; Wetlands coast/grove/dig site/Thandol. Treat these as *artwork-area* pins unless a same-build live waypoint exists.
3. For later route places, keep the already distinct `route-N` planning markers but avoid calling them quest-object locations. Acquire actual quest IDs, giver/objective records and screenshots before subdividing vague “circuit” entries. Preserve explicit unplaced status for Desolace route 3, Feralas route 2, new Wetlands pickups, Ruins staging, and off-map Blackrock travel.
4. Verify art pixel QA on each source image, especially pin-to-landmark alignment; test visible marker count/selection on desktop and touch, navigation from one cluster to **more than one** objective, and shared quest status. Keep entrance pins on the correct surface or instance map. Do not convert old Classic `/way` comments or WCLBox service coordinates to supposedly precise Forever quest-object pixels.
