# Explicit zone visits and dungeon navigation

Reviewed 8 October 2026.

The sidebar previously rendered `STAGES` only. Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron and Deadmines were the only dedicated dungeon stages; the remaining 31 sections were nested in zone schedules. The sidebar now links all 34 existing run sections once, beside their scheduled stages, preserving the opening run order and completion state. Dungeon wings and scoped BRD sections remain separate checklist links under the existing one-clear policy.

The original route mainly represented each zone as one broad level band. It mentioned returns as alternatives or circuit notes rather than navigable visits.

## Online inspiration

- [Sage Guide](https://www.sageguide.net/) explicitly revisits Duskwood (20–23, 27–28), Redridge (19–20, 28–29), Stranglethorn (32, 36–37, 40–42), and Tanaris (43–44, 48–49). This is the main structural inspiration: separate visits, level windows, and deferred chain stages. Its complete speedrun order is not copied into this dungeon-focused route.
- [Judgement's Alliance guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/alliance-leveling-guide/) search excerpts show separate early Loch Modan/Redridge brackets and Westfall/Stormwind messenger turn-ins. The full page returned HTTP 403 during research; no detailed itinerary was derived from inaccessible content.
- [Joana's guide manual](https://www.joanasworld.com/azeroth.php) describes speed-leveling routes and selective quest omission. This guide likewise treats completed, grey, blocked, and inefficient leftovers as skippable rather than requiring full zone completion.

## Adaptation

Six new stages expose the short early Lakeshire Defias trip; Westfall after Ruins; Duskwood and Redridge after Wetlands; northern Stranglethorn after the Desolace/Monastery block; and Tanaris after Feralas/Zul'Farrak. Existing stage IDs, step indexes, quest IDs and dungeon rules are retained. Revisit pages reuse the earlier curated quest statuses and show earlier notes. New notes and route checks are keyed by the visit ID.

Level windows are planning targets adapted to the existing Forever dungeon schedule, not verified Forever XP benchmarks. New planning areas have explicit unplaced reasons rather than invented coordinates. Existing hubs remain available on the return maps; curated pickup lists reuse the same quest IDs. The short early Lakeshire visit does not expose the later Redridge combat quest package.

The six new visits are conditional on useful unfinished objectives. Leave triggers explain when to continue; players ahead from dungeon XP can skip filler without repeating dungeon clears.
